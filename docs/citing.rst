Citing and license
==================

If RDEFpy helps your work, please cite the poster that introduced it:

   Togni, A., Malphrus, B., Arnold, B., Mayo, D., Sanchez-Net, M., & Asmar,
   S. (2026). Extending VLBI Station Capabilities for Delta-DOR and
   Navigation via Open-Source RDEF Software Compliant with CCSDS Standards.
   Poster, 14th General Meeting of the International VLBI Service for
   Geodesy and Astrometry (IVS GM2026), Würzburg.
   https://indico.astro.uni-wuerzburg.de/event/2/contributions/143/

The carrier estimator in :func:`rdefpy.extract_carrier` is described in:

   Togni, A., Zannoni, M., & Tortora, P. (2025). Accurate frequency
   estimation through iterative parabolic interpolations. *Signal
   Processing*, 237, 110094. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sigpro.2025.110094

The format is defined in CCSDS 506.1-B-1, *Delta-DOR Raw Data Exchange
Format*, Blue Book, June 2013, also published as ISO 20208:2015. DSN's
version of the product record, with its DSN-specific fields, is DSN 820-013,
module 0222-Science, *Open Loop Data Interface*, Rev. A, 5 June 2019, JPL
D-76531, Jet Propulsion Laboratory,
https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/radiosciencedocs/urn-nasa-pds-radiosci_documentation/dsn_0222-science/dsn_0222-science.2019-06-05.pdf
